01 / Perspective

Look out far enough, and the horizon begins to bend.

Earth is almost a sphere, gently flattened at its poles. From the ground, its vastness can feel endless; from high above, its curve gathers everything we know into one luminous home. That change in perspective is a reminder: we belong to something both immense and shared.

02 / The living angle

There is no single way to meet a world.

Stand on a hillside, sail toward a far shore, or watch daylight trace the edge of the planet: the curve meets every viewpoint at a different angle. Life does something similar. We each see the world from where we are, yet our paths share the same ground, water, and air.

Our differences are real. So is our connection.

03 / Time in bloom

Life keeps finding a way to begin again.

Across billions of years, small beginnings became a branching, changing abundance. Every chapter builds on what came before—not as a straight line, but as a living, interwoven story.

~3.5 billion years ago

First traces

Early life takes hold in ancient waters, beginning a story that will never stop changing.

~2.4 billion years ago

Air transforms

Oxygen begins to build in the atmosphere, reshaping the possibilities for life.

~500 million years ago

Life diversifies

Many forms flourish in the seas, and life’s branches spread into new expressions.

Today

Our chapter

We are part of the story now—with the chance to care for what comes next.

04 / Still becoming

Wonder is a way of belonging.

Every breath joins an ancient exchange. Every life touches other lives. On this curved, remarkable Earth, we are not just looking at the story—we are helping it grow.

Made for the one world we share.
