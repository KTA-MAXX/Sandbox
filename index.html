Notes from the bunch

More than
just a snack.

A few handy facts to make your next banana break a little more interesting.

01 / THE PLANT

Not a tree.
Not even close.

The banana plant is a giant herb. Its tall, trunk-like stem is made of tightly wrapped leaf stalks, and it can grow several metres high.

02 / THE BERRY

Plot twist:
it’s a berry.

Botanically speaking, bananas are berries. Strawberries, despite the name, are not. Fruit classification has a sense of humour.

03 / THE RIPENING

Green to gold,
on its own time.

As a banana ripens, its starches turn into sugars. A paper bag can help it along by keeping the natural ripening gas, ethylene, nearby.

04 / THE FOOD

Portable
plant power.

A medium banana has about 105 calories, 3 grams of fibre, and 422 milligrams of potassium. Nature packed the snack for you.

The amazing banana

One plant.
Many hands.

Most familiar dessert bananas are seedless and sterile. Farmers grow new plants from shoots that sprout near the base, so the next generation is a genetic copy of its parent.
