Not separate from nature

Earth is not
the backdrop.
It is the relationship.

A breath of air, a glass of water, a meal shared at the table: each is a small meeting with the living planet.

Water moves between ocean, cloud, soil, leaf, and body. Carbon passes through air and living things. Sunlight becomes warmth, then food, then energy for a day of work or rest. We experience Earth not as a distant globe, but as the conditions that let ordinary life happen.

And life changes those conditions in return. Forests shape local water cycles. Tiny ocean organisms help move carbon. Human choices change the atmosphere we all share. The connection goes both ways.

A very long becoming

Life took its time.

Every chapter made the next one possible. Here is a tiny window into the story so far.

  1. 4.54 billion years ago

    A planet gathers

    Dust and rock orbiting the young Sun collide and collect into Earth.

  2. At least 3.5 billion years ago

    Life leaves traces

    Ancient rocks preserve evidence of early life, likely tiny and single-celled.

  3. About 2.4 billion years ago

    The air begins to change

    Oxygen from photosynthetic microbes builds up in the atmosphere over a long stretch of time.

  4. About 541 million years ago

    Life diversifies

    Many kinds of complex animal life appear in the fossil record during the Cambrian period.

  5. About 300,000 years ago

    Here we are

    Homo sapiens emerge, carrying a very recent chapter in an ancient story.

Dates are approximate. Earth science keeps refining the details as new evidence comes to light.
